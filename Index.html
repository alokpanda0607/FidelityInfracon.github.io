About Us

Technical Compliance Framework

The 21 Gates of Technical Compliance & NOC

A structured 21-gate system ensuring your project meets every technical, statutory and regulatory requirement — before, during and after construction.

GATES 1–7

Phase 1

Pre-Construction: Planning & Statutory Alignment
  • Ownership & Title Clearance
  • T&CP Approval • Fire NOC (Provisional)
  • Environmental & Height Clearance
  • Municipal Sanction (OBPAS)
  • RERA Project Registration
GATES 8–14

Phase 2

Construction-Phase: Technical Governance
  • Water Abstraction & Telemetry Setup
  • Structural Stability Vetting
  • MEP Clash Detection Audit
  • Electrical Sanction (CEIG)
  • STP Consent & Fire Pressure Logs
GATES 15–21

Phase 3

Post-Construction: Closeout & Capital Exit
  • Lift & Escalator License
  • Pollution Control Board CTO
  • Final Electrical Energization
  • As-Built Drawing Reconciliation
  • Final Fire NOC & Occupancy Certificate
